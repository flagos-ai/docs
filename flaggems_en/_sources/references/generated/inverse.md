# inverse

**Kind:** LinearAlg | **Stage:** alpha | **Since:** 5.4

## Description

Computes the inverse of a square matrix, batched over the leading dimensions
and equivalent to torch.inverse. Small real matrices use a fused pivoted
Gauss-Jordan Triton kernel; larger real matrices and complex inputs are
delegated to the vendor (cuSOLVER) inverse.
