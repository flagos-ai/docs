# Stage 0: Business Kickoff and Contracting

**Goal**

Establish the cooperation relationship and clarify the responsibilities of both parties.

**Prerequisites**

The chip company has a base SDK/driver and can provide a development environment.

**Activities**

1. Sign an MOU defining the cooperation goals, adaptation scope, and division of responsibilities.
2. Connect with the FlagOS cooperation manager and assign contacts for both parties.
3. Set the initial target level for chip adaptation, usually starting at the intermediate level.
4. Sign the Contributor License Agreement (CLA).

**Deliverables**

Signed MOU and contact list for both parties.

**FlagOS contacts**

Dedicated cooperation manager and technical architect.

**Chip company contacts**

Project director and technical lead.

**Acceptance criteria**

MOU signed and contacts confirmed by both parties.

**Estimated duration**

1-2 weeks

**Related documents**

MOU template, CLA, and cooperation handbook
