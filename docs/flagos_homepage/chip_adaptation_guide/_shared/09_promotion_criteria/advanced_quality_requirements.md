# Advanced Cooperation: Deterministic Performance and Quality Requirements

- Performance metrics are prerequisites for advanced cooperation, Day 0 plans, distribution validation, and showroom display; they must not be treated as post-release promotional items.

- Vendors must maintain the mainstream diff so that chip adaptation on the mainline has continuous ownership.

- FlagOS upgrades and vendor Driver/SDK upgrades must both enter CI/CD validation.

- Day 0 releases, distribution plans, and policy recommendation plans must meet maturity, support, performance, and quality requirements.
