# Requirements for promotion from basic to intermediate

- The FlagTree chip branch runs stably for at least four weeks

- FlagGems operator test coverage is at least 80%

- The CI single machine is in the FlagOS resource pool with availability of at least 99%

- The chip passes Sig repository functional validation.

- Establish an SBOM scanning mechanism

- The driver/SDK can be obtained unattended

- Sign and pass legal compliance review
